Course case study (fictional company)

FocusVu: Classroom Launch Strategy

A SMART marketing plan for the launch of a classroom feature by FocusVu, a fictional B2B video demo software company from a university marketing course. This is a learning project, not client work.

SMART goal: Within 60 days of launching the new classroom feature, get 25% of FocusVu's active customer accounts to host at least one classroom session, using LinkedIn, YouTube, webinars and email.
KPITarget
Classroom adoption rate25% of active accounts within 60 days
Launch email engagement40% open rate, 8% click rate
Qualified leads60 leads at no more than $150 each

Includes: business description, situation analysis, SMART goal, KPIs and a channel plan across LinkedIn, YouTube, webinars and email.

Creative concept

Asien Market: 45-Second Launch Ad

A vertical (9:16) video concept for Asien Market, an Asian grocery store at Friheden Shoppingcenter in Hvidovre, where I work part-time. The plan is a nine-scene storyboard that mixes real store photos with a few AI-generated support visuals, with voiceover, on-screen text and a CapCut edit guide.

WhatDetail
Format45 seconds, 9:16, for Instagram, TikTok and Facebook
ApproachReal photos first. AI visuals only as support. The logo and packaging are never altered.
Message"Your Asian kitchen, close to home," with click and collect
RulesNo invented prices, discounts or product claims

This is a concept and production plan. The final ad is not yet published.
