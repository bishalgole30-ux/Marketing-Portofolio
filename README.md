## Projects

### 1. FocusVu: Classroom Launch Strategy
*University marketing course case study (fictional company, not client work)*

A SMART marketing plan for launching a classroom feature for a B2B video demo software company.

- **Goal:** Within 60 days of launch, get 25% of active customer accounts to host at least one classroom session.
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, YouTube, webinars and email.
- **KPIs:** 25% classroom adoption rate, 40% email open rate and 8% click rate, and 60 qualified leads at no more than $150 each.
- **Covers:** business description, situation analysis, SMART goal, KPIs and marketing activities.

### 2. Asien Market: 45-Second Launch Ad
*Creative concept for a real local store where I work part-time*

A vertical (9:16) video ad plan for an Asian grocery store at Friheden Shoppingcenter, Hvidovre.

- **Approach:** real store photos first, AI visuals only as support, original logo and packaging never altered.
- **Deliverables:** nine-scene storyboard, voiceover script, on-screen text and an edit guide for CapCut.
- **Message:** "Your Asian kitchen, close to home," with a click-and-collect call to action.
- **Rule followed:** no invented prices, discounts or product claims.
